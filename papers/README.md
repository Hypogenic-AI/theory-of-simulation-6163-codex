# Downloaded Papers

1. [Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior](generative_agents_interactive_simulacra_of_human_behavior.pdf)
   - Authors: Joon Sung Park, Joseph C. O'Brien, Carrie J. Cai, Meredith Ringel Morris, Percy Liang, Michael S. Bernstein
   - Year: 2023
   - arXiv: 2304.03442
   - Why relevant: Foundational agent architecture for believable human-behavior simulation with memory, reflection, and planning.

2. [Social Simulacra: Creating Populated Prototypes for Social Computing Systems](social_simulacra_creating_populated_prototypes.pdf)
   - Authors: Joon Sung Park, Lindsay Popowski, Carrie J. Cai, Meredith Ringel Morris, Percy Liang, Michael S. Bernstein
   - Year: 2022
   - arXiv: 2208.04024
   - Why relevant: Early prompt-based population simulation work for large-scale social interactions.

3. [Using Large Language Models to Simulate Multiple Humans and Replicate Human Subject Studies](using_llms_to_simulate_multiple_humans.pdf)
   - Authors: Gati Aher, Rosa I. Arriaga, Adam Tauman Kalai
   - Year: 2023
   - arXiv: 2208.10264
   - Why relevant: Introduces Turing Experiments as a concrete evaluation framework for human-behavior simulation fidelity.

4. [Whose Opinions Do Language Models Reflect?](whose_opinions_do_language_models_reflect.pdf)
   - Authors: Shibani Santurkar, Esin Durmus, Faisal Ladhak, Cinoo Lee, Percy Liang, Tatsunori Hashimoto
   - Year: 2023
   - arXiv: 2303.17548
   - Why relevant: Provides OpinionQA and a quantitative framework for demographic opinion alignment.

5. [S^3: Social-network Simulation System with Large Language Model-Empowered Agents](s3_social_network_simulation_system.pdf)
   - Authors: Lei Wang et al.
   - Year: 2023
   - arXiv: 2307.14984
   - Why relevant: Multi-agent social-network simulation system relevant to emergent collective behavior.

6. [LLMs Simulate Big Five Personality Traits: Further Evidence](llms_simulate_big_five_personality_traits.pdf)
   - Authors: Aleksandra Sorokovikova, Natalia Fedorova, Sharwin Rezagholi, Ivan P. Yamshchikov
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2402.01765
   - Why relevant: Tests whether LLM behavior can be parameterized by stable personality structure.

7. [Large language models as linguistic simulators and cognitive models in human research](llms_as_linguistic_simulators_and_cognitive_models.pdf)
   - Author: Zhicheng Lin
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2402.04470
   - Why relevant: Conceptual paper arguing for simulation and modeling rather than replacement of human subjects.

8. [Can Large Language Model Agents Simulate Human Trust Behavior?](can_llm_agents_simulate_human_trust_behaviors.pdf)
   - Authors: Chengxing Xie et al.
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2402.04559
   - Why relevant: Strong empirical test of behavioral alignment in Trust Games; useful as a benchmark template.

9. [Large Language Models Assume People are More Rational than We Really Are](llms_assume_people_are_more_rational.pdf)
   - Authors: Ryan Liu, Jiayi Geng, Joshua C. Peterson, Ilia Sucholutsky, Thomas L. Griffiths
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2406.17055
   - Why relevant: Directly identifies a systematic distortion in LLM human simulation: rationality overestimation.

10. [Towards Real-world Human Behavior Simulation: Benchmarking Large Language Models on Long-horizon, Cross-scenario, Heterogeneous Behavior Traces](omnibehavior_benchmarking_llms_on_real_world_human_behavior.pdf)
   - Authors: Jiawei Chen et al.
   - Year: 2026
   - arXiv: 2604.08362
   - Why relevant: Most recent benchmark paper found in this pass; highlights long-horizon and cross-scenario failure modes.
